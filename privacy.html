Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

CloudCove Web ("CloudCove Web," "we," "us," or "our") builds custom websites and provides a 24/7 AI receptionist service that answers calls and captures leads for local businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide when booking a call, filling out a form, or contacting us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and business details.
  • Call data processed by our AI receptionist, including call recordings, transcripts, and caller-provided details, used to answer calls and capture leads on behalf of our business clients.
  • Website usage data such as pages visited, browser type, device information, and referral source, collected automatically through standard analytics tools.
  • Communications you send us, including emails and messages, which we may retain to respond to you and improve our services.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our website and AI receptionist services.
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule calls, and deliver customer support.
  • To improve our services, including training and tuning the AI receptionist to better handle calls.
  • To send service-related updates and, where permitted, marketing communications you can opt out of at any time.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our website, hosting, analytics, communications, and AI voice infrastructure, under confidentiality obligations.
  • The business client on whose behalf a call was answered, when you interact with our AI receptionist for that business.
  • Authorities, when required to comply with the law or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

5. Your Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below.
  • You may opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link included in those messages.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact us at info@cloudcoveweb.com.
